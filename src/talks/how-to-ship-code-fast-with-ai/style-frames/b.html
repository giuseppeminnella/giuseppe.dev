B · drift, the team, the cost, everywhere, with AI, when to go fast, the morning after

Drift is the gap between what you meant and what you got.

A team is many of these. Different starting points, different drifts, one feature.

Every gap has to be closed before the feature ships. That is the cost of alignment, and it is never a straight line.

Everyone feeds the feature, directly or not: other teams, legal, the company, the industry. And everyone can drift.

The dashed lines are the ideal path. Nobody follows them. Every way back costs time and resources.

Back to one line. Drift was always there. Now add AI.

It is fast. It goes too far, circles the target and never lands.

So you stop, branch from a good point and go again. Cheap lines, many of them, closer every time: a game of approximation.

Until one lands. Then that line consolidates.

So is drift bad? Not necessarily. Done on purpose, it is exploration: it is how you found the line.

Drift helps in some places and costs in others. So: when do you go fast, and when do you slow down?

Just for you: try anything, throw it away. For millions of users: one careful line.

The morning after. Drift that reached the slow corners leaves scars, and they reach everyone, even the script that was just for you.
