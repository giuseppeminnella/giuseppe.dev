On 22 October I spoke at [buildersklub](https://luma.com/builde-xnpy), at the Deutsche Bank Berlin Technology Centre. It was the fourth edition of [Engineering as containment](/posts/engineering-as-containment-bologna-talk/), and the first one you can go through yourself: the presentation above runs in your browser, click by click, the way I gave it.

The argument fits in two lines. Drift is the gap between what you meant and what you got. **AI makes expansion cheap. Engineering must make acceptance expensive.**

## What is in it

<!-- TODO(Giuseppe): one or two sentences per chapter, or cut to the ones you want to stress. -->

- **Drift happens everywhere.** From one person to the team, the squad, the company and the industry, and every way back costs alignment.
- **With AI.** The line gets faster, overshoots and branches. Drift on purpose is exploration; drift by accident is a cost.
- **When to go fast.** It depends on who depends on it and what breaks if it is wrong. Go crazy at home, go slow where the scars are.
- **Where the time goes.** Writing code is a small slice of a developer's week. Making it faster, or instant, moves the queue to review.
- **Silos.** Every area of work is a profession, and every profession is a silo. Tooling can narrow the distance.
- **Solutions.** Contain the drift in practice, in tooling and in people: one thread at a time, changes that fit in a human head.
- **The software factory.** A window into the future, why it is not for us yet, and something you can try today.
